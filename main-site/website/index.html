CAPITAL ALIGNMENT

50:50 Senior / Subordinated Fund Product

Lumen initially matches senior capital 1:1 after deduction of the 2% upfront management fee. Subordinated capital absorbs losses first; senior capital incurs losses only after subordinated capital is exhausted.

Target returns are allocation benchmarks, not promised returns. Once both base allocations are satisfied, excess profits are split 30% to senior and 70% to subordinated capital. Refer to the final subscription documents for the Carry threshold, 20% / 30% rates, reversal rules and all other detailed terms.

Investment may result in a loss of principal. This product structure does not guarantee capital or promise returns.
